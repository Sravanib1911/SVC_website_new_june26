Learning formats

Three formats. Built for how you learn.

Everyone learns differently — so we deliver the same outcome-driven curriculum in the format that fits your life. Join a structured live cohort, move at your own pace with full mentor support, or roll out a managed learning platform for your team. Whichever you choose, the content, projects and standards stay identical, and every path ends in real, portfolio-ready work.

Live cohort

Twice-weekly instructor-led sessions with peers, weekly office hours and a moderated capstone showcase.

Self-paced

Recorded modules, graded laboratory assignments and an active learner community — designed for working professionals.

LMS

A managed learning platform for your engineering and data teams — custom curricula, progress tracking and assessments against your own data and use cases.
