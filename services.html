Common questions

Frequently asked questions

How quickly can an engagement begin?

Discovery typically commences within one week of contract execution. Most engagements deliver a working prototype within two to four weeks.

Do you support on-premises and customer-VPC deployments?

Yes. SVC deploys within your cloud account, your customer VPC, or fully on-premises where data-residency requirements demand it.

How are data privacy and compliance managed?

All engagements operate under ISO 27001 and SOC 2 aligned controls by default. HIPAA, PCI-DSS and GDPR scopes are supported on a per-engagement basis with full documentation.

Who owns the intellectual property we develop together?

You retain full ownership of all custom code, trained models and data artefacts produced for your engagement. SVC's shared platform components remain licensed for ongoing use.
