Our mission

Making AI useful, durable and accountable — for every enterprise that depends on it.

We believe the true value of AI is realised at the edge of the business — in revenue gained, cost reduced and risk mitigated. That conviction shapes every engagement: senior teams, clearly contracted outcomes, and engineering discipline robust enough to withstand production.

The name SVC stands for Strategic Vision Coder — a deliberate expression of how we operate: strategy, applied research and engineering, unified within a single accountable team.

What we stand for

Four operating principles.

Engagements are contracted against commercial KPIs — revenue, cost, risk — not features shipped.

Responsible by default

Fairness, explainability and security are engineered in from day one — aligned to ISO 27001 and SOC 2 controls.

Our journey

A brief corporate history.

  1. Pre-2026

    Foundational engagements

    The founding team delivers a portfolio of AI, data and software engagements across financial services, healthcare and consumer technology — building the operating discipline that would later define the firm.

  2. 2026 · Q1

    Incorporated as a Private Limited entity

    SVC Tech AI Solutions Private Limited is formally registered in India, establishing the governance, compliance and commercial foundations for institutional engagements.

  3. 2026 · Q2

    SVC Academy launched

    Formalised the practitioner-led education programme — covering Data Science and Web Development tracks — with a structured paid internship pathway for graduating cohorts.

  4. 2026 · Q3

    SVC Product Suite released

    Commercial release of the proprietary product portfolio spanning data platforms, agentic copilots, computer vision, voice and decision intelligence.

  5. Present

    An integrated AI practice

    Today SVC operates a unified practice — Products, Services and Education — serving enterprise and mid-market clients across 7+ countries.
